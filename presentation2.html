Literature Survey

What Research Says

1

WHO — Pharma Supply Chain Study

Manual inventory systems cause 20–30% stock discrepancy rates in small pharmaceutical distributors.

2

Journal of Business Logistics (2022)

Digital B2B ordering portals reduce processing time by 55–65% compared to manual order systems.

3

GSTN Guidelines — India (2023)

Pharma invoices must include batch number, expiry date, HSN code, CGST/SGST split — impossible to guarantee manually.

Literature Survey

More Key Findings

4

IEEE — Real-Time Database Systems (2021)

Concurrent order conflicts cause stock mismatches — only atomic DB transactions prevent over-commitment.

5

NCBI — RBAC in Healthcare Systems (2020)

Role-Based Access Control is critical — owner and retailer must have strictly separated data permissions.

Conclusion: All 5 sources confirm — small pharma distributors using manual systems face stock inaccuracy, GST non-compliance, and order delays. A real-time digital portal directly solves all three.
Existing System Analysis

Current System at JRG Medical Agencies

📒

Manual Log Books

Handwritten stock registers, order books, and payment notebooks — no digital record.

LIMITATIONS

  • Stock figures updated only after manual log entry
  • Two retailers can order same stock simultaneously — no conflict protection
  • Order history only accessible by physically searching notebooks

🧾

Handwritten Invoices

  • GST calculation done manually — prone to arithmetic errors
  • CGST/SGST split, batch number, expiry date all filled by hand
  • 30+ minutes per invoice during peak hours

💳

Payment & Credit Tracking

  • Outstanding dues tracked in separate paper ledgers
  • No instant visibility — requires manual search at end of day
Existing System Analysis

Other Available Solutions & Why They Don't Fit

📊

Pharma ERP / Tally

Desktop accounting software used by larger distributors

  • Desktop-only — no web or mobile access for retailers
  • High cost (₹8,000–₹25,000/year) — not viable for small agencies
  • No retailer self-service — all entries must be done by owner
  • No promotional scheme automation

🌐

B2B Pharma Platforms (e.g. Pharmarack)

Online ordering platforms for large distributors

  • Designed for large-scale distributors — not customizable for small agencies
  • Monthly subscription fees not suitable for small business budgets
  • No support for custom promotional schemes specific to JRG's business
  • No custom invoice format with agency-specific GSTIN details
⚠️   No existing solution fits a small pharmaceutical distributor needing a custom, affordable, role-based portal.

Proposed System

What We Are Building

A full-stack web portal for JRG Medical Agencies — giving retailers a digital storefront to browse & order, and the owner a live dashboard to manage everything — replacing all manual log books with an automated, real-time system.

🛍️ Retailer Side

  • Browse full product catalog with live stock
  • Add to cart — scheme auto-calculated
  • Place order → instant stock deduction
  • Download GST invoice immediately

📊 Owner Side

  • Add / manage products & stock levels
  • View all retailer orders in real time
  • Track payment dues per retailer
  • Send notifications to retailers
